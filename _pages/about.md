Hello! My name is Nicholas Jones, a graduate student at Southern Methodist University Master of Science in Data Science program, with a focus on quantitative analysis and AI/ machine learning applications in finance and healthcare. Today, I'm passionate about financial modeling, algorithmic modeling, and qualitative evaluation across all sectors, yet my main focus remains in Biotech and drug discovery/ innovation.
